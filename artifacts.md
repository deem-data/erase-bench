Trained, retrained, and unlearned models can be found here: [https://huggingface.co/datasets/deem-data/erase-bench/tree/main/saved](https://huggingface.co/datasets/deem-data/erase-bench/tree/main/saved). To use models for unlearning or evaluation copy them to erase-bench/saved/.
